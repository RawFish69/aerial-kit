Powered-flight accelerometer policy and vertical telemetry
Date: 2026-11-18
Origin: docs/evidence/fc-sil-wing-estimator-finding-2026-11-18.md

Wing estimator:
  - Accelerometer levelling remains enabled before launch/disarmed, where the
    aircraft is actually resting in gravity.
  - Powered flight uses gyro-carried attitude for wings. The portable core has
    no airspeed/GPS acceleration input with which to subtract longitudinal or
    centripetal specific force.
  - Multirotors retain the existing small-angle correction and gyro-carried
    attitude beyond seven degrees.

Verification:
  - Physical-accelerometer wing regression now XPASSes: both wings remain
    within 5 degrees of level and above 50 m after 6 s.
  - Gravity-only wing control still passes.
  - Physical-accelerometer quad sustained-tilt regression still passes.
  - Host flight-core self-test: 22/22.
  - ARM build and binary image check pass.

Vertical radio telemetry:
  - CRSF type 0x09 BARO_ALTITUDE/VARIO codec added with big-endian altitude and
    vertical speed.
  - Independent wire fixture: altitude 123.4 m, vario -2.5 m/s.
  - ROS receiver/backend integration is requested in
    docs/evidence/change-requests.md so the EKF can use /uav/hw/baro.

Known hardware limitation:
  - Gyro-carried attitude is exact in the bias-free simulator but will drift
    on hardware. A thrust-model specific-force compensation or a carefully
    gated slow re-levelling policy remains required before claiming long-flight
    attitude accuracy. No flight-readiness claim is made.
