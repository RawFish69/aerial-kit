AK multirotor accelerometer policy
Date: 2026-11-18
Origin: docs/evidence/fc-sil-estimator-finding-2026-11-17.md

Policy:
  - A disarmed multirotor keeps accelerometer attitude levelling for boot and
    pre-arm convergence.
  - An armed multirotor keeps the accelerometer only while estimated roll and
    pitch are below 7 degrees, with a 0.2 s time constant. At larger angles the
    gyro carries attitude because rotor specific force no longer represents
    gravity.
  - A fixed wing keeps the existing 0.5 s accelerometer aid in flight.
