Twin-wing airframe refinement: shared spec, articulated MuJoCo model, Gazebo parity
Measured 2026-11-15. MuJoCo 4.0.1, ROS 2 Jazzy, Gazebo Sim 8.10.0.

See docs/TWIN_WING_AIRFRAME.md for the design and the integrated / articulated
/ visual / approximate breakdown.

7. Still approximate / open

  - Aero coefficients remain research defaults, not fitted to this geometry.
  - Visual meshes produce no forces; no hinge-moment or prop-torque aero.
  - Prop shaft speed is display-scaled (angular momentum preserved).
  - Gazebo is a kinematic visual: elevons neutral, props static.
  - The elevon convention was resolved after the handoff: simulator indices
    2/3 are now physically left/right everywhere, matching the firmware
    document. The allocator, aero differential and teleop signs were changed
    together so the physical response is unchanged.
  - No hardware readiness or real-aircraft validation is claimed.
