4. Failure paths
  No display: teleop prints "cannot open the Tk key window ... nothing was
  armed. Exiting.", the launch shuts the session down, no processes remain.
  log: teleop-no-display.log (adapter was interrupted during its own import,
  so its traceback there is shutdown noise, not a flight error).
  Teleop killed (SIGTERM): disarms, exits cleanly, launch shuts down.

5. Fixed during this work
  - Adapter exit with the viewer open segfaulted (-11): close() only asks
    the viewer thread to stop. Now the render thread is joined first;
    shutdown reports "finished cleanly".
  - Adapter printed ExternalShutdownException on launch shutdown; it now
    exits normally.
  - Independent review reran the live XTEST session and caught a narrower
    shutdown race: the ROS context could become invalid between a timer tick
    and its final Telemetry publish, producing an RCLError traceback. The
    adapter now checks the context before the tick and treats a context loss
    during publish as teardown. The repeated live session completed with both
    nodes finishing cleanly and no leftover processes.
  - The MuJoCo viewer takes keyboard focus from the teleop window when it
    opens; the teleop now reclaims focus once after 3 s.

B. NEEDS HUMAN VISUAL CONFIRMATION

  The key path, signs and measured responses above are automated. These are
  judgements a person should make by flying it:
  - The elevons visibly swing about their hinge lines in the viewer for
    W/S/A/D (screenshots show the deflected surfaces, not the motion).
  - The props visibly speed up/slow down with Space/Shift and Q/E.
  - Handling feels controllable at ±10 deg full stick.
  - Focus reclaim works under your window manager (otherwise click the
    teleop window).
  - Real keyboard auto-repeat feels right (the filter is unit-tested and
    exercised by held XTEST keys, not by a physical keyboard).

C. LIMITS
  Open-loop actuator control, no stabilisation; unfitted aero; digital
  keys; no landing/takeoff model; truth state only in teleop mode. No
  hardware or RC-link readiness is claimed.
