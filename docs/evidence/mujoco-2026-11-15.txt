MuJoCo twin-wing physics and ROS path
Measured 2026-11-15. MuJoCo 4.0.1, ROS 2 Jazzy.

1. Plant

  sim_py/backends/mujoco_fixedwing_backend.py

  - MuJoCo owns the free root body and the two elevon hinges.
  - The wing, fuselage, fins, elevons and prop discs form one MJCF body tree.
  - Aerial Kit's fixed-wing model supplies lift, drag, thrust and moments.
  - Actual hinge positions feed the next aero step.
  - Thrust has a configurable first-order lag.
  - Two prop joints animate from the commanded thrust.
  - World-frame wind remains available through the same FixedWingDynamics path.

  This is not claiming native MuJoCo fluid lift. MuJoCo is the rigid-body and
  articulated-surface integrator; Aerial Kit supplies the aerodynamic loads.

3. ROS end-to-end

  ros2 launch sim_fast fixed_wing_mujoco_demo.launch.py \
    start_delay_sec:=2.0 wind_x_mps:=3.0 wind_y_mps:=1.0

  The launched adapter reported:
    fixed-wing backend: mujoco, z=55.0 m, airspeed=15.0 m/s

  Waypoints:
    first                [100.1, 48.1, 53.5]
    second               [208.1, 61.7, 54.9]
    third                [257.1, -28.6, 53.6]
    mission complete     [257.13, -28.60, 53.59]
    final distance       10.33 m

  The same fixed_wing_guidance_node and fixed_wing_demo_node run above the
  analytic and MuJoCo plants. The adapter backend parameter selects:
    analytic  - fast fixed-step integration
    mujoco    - MuJoCo rigid-body and hinge integration

  MuJoCo viewer is optional:
    ros2 launch sim_fast fixed_wing_mujoco_demo.launch.py viewer:=true

4. ROS with simulated sensor estimation

  ros2 launch sim_fast fixed_wing_mujoco_demo.launch.py \
    start_delay_sec:=2.0 wind_x_mps:=3.0 wind_y_mps:=1.0 \
    estimator_mode:=ekf estimator_seed:=7

  The adapter inserts deterministic GPS, baro and accelerometer noise through
  Aerial Kit's SimulatedIns/INS EKF, and guidance consumes the estimate rather
  than the MuJoCo truth state.

  Waypoints:
    first                [102.2, 48.4, 53.7]
    second               [209.5, 65.6, 54.1]
    third                [257.2, -28.5, 54.5]
    mission complete     [257.23, -28.45, 54.47]
    final distance       9.35 m

  This is deterministic for estimator_seed and uses the same launch and
  /uav/* contract as the truth-backed run.

  SimState and Telemetry now carry air-relative airspeed explicitly. Both the
  fixed-wing controller and AirspeedNav inner loop use that measurement instead
  of treating horizontal ground speed as airspeed; the estimator passes the
  measured value through beside its estimated position and velocity.
