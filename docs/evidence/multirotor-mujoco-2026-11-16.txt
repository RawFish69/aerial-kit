C. LIMITS
  Lagged-command rotor thrust (no RPM^2/voltage/inflow/ground effect), linear
  drag, research-default inertias (micro: CAD + assumed battery), display-rate
  rotor shafts, simulator flight modes (not a Betaflight/PX4 port), no Gazebo
  models generated for the new airframes. No hardware readiness claimed.
