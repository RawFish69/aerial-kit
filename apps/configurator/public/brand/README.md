# AerialKit logo — original Rotor A

The selected logo is the original 01 Rotor A: a swept-wing A with a three-blade
propeller in its counter. The app header and favicon use `aerialkit-rotor-a.svg`.

See [the branding guide](../../docs/BRANDING.md) for the shared firmware and configurator identity.

## Current assets

- `aerialkit-rotor-a.svg`: Blueprint blue #4c90f0; app header and favicon.
- `aerialkit-rotor-a-white.svg`: light monochrome for dark surfaces.
- `aerialkit-rotor-a-ink.svg`: dark monochrome for light surfaces.
- `aerialkit-rotor-a-logo-light.svg` and `aerialkit-rotor-a-logo-dark.svg`: selected horizontal wordmarks.

The mark has a 64 × 64 viewBox. Use it at 16 px for the favicon and 28–32 px
in the app. Preserve its proportions and leave at least 8 px between the mark
and adjacent navbar text. It works in one colour, without gradients or effects.

## Earlier explorations

The plain swept-wing A, Wing + rotor and version 2 remain as separate SVGs.
Version 2 was reviewed and declined; it is not the app logo. The comparison
`aerialkit-rotor-a-refinement.svg` records the original on the left and version
2 on the right. Existing v2 wordmarks are exploration assets, not selected ones.
